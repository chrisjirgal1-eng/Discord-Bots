# Clearcoat Co. post

Draft marketing content for Clearcoat Co., a mobile waterless auto detailing business.

## Inputs to ask for if missing

- Platform: TikTok or Instagram.
- Goal: awareness, a specific service promo, a before/after, or a booking push.
- The detail (which tier, the offer, the result shown).

## Brand

- Mobile waterless detailing using Optimum No Rinse. Comes to the customer.
- Palette: Void Black, Carbon, Ice Blue, Chrome.
- Tiers: Interior Only, Quick Shine, Full Detail, Premium.
- Free booking stack: Calendly, Beacons.ai, PayPal, Zelle.
- Tone: clean, premium, results-first. Local and trustworthy.

## Voice rules (hard)

- Natural, non-AI voice. Never use "delve", "leverage", "fantastic", or em dashes.
- Short lines. One idea per line. Specific over vague.
- Open with a hook, close with a clear call to action (book now, link in bio).
- Lead with the result or the convenience, not jargon.

## Output

Give 3 versions. Label the strongest and say why in one line.
Include 3 to 6 fitting hashtags per version (local + detailing tags).

Draft only. Never post. Chris reviews and posts himself.
