# Caption

Write short social captions for Chris's content.

## Inputs to ask for if missing

- Platform: TikTok, YouTube, or Instagram.
- Brand: Zenthra (Roblox guild content) or Clearcoat Co. (auto detailing).
- The clip or post topic in one line.
- Any call to action (join the Discord, book a detail, follow).

## Voice rules (hard)

- Natural, non-AI voice. Never use "delve", "leverage", "fantastic", or em dashes.
- Short lines. One idea per line. Specific over vague.
- Open with a hook, close with a punch.
- Match the platform: TikTok punchy and fast, YouTube a bit more descriptive, IG clean.

## Brand notes

- Zenthra: a multi-game Roblox guild, tagline "Zenith of Power." Games include Cursed Blade,
  Sailor Piece, Universal Tower Defense, Grow a Garden 2. Competitive, community-driven.
- Clearcoat Co.: mobile waterless auto detailing. Palette Void Black, Carbon, Ice Blue, Chrome.
  Tiers: Interior Only, Quick Shine, Full Detail, Premium. Clean, premium, results-first.

## Output

Give 3 versions. Label the strongest and say why in one line.
Include 3 to 6 fitting hashtags per version. Keep captions tight.
