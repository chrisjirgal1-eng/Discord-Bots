# Zenthra announcement

Draft a Discord announcement for the Zenthra guild (1,300+ members).

## Inputs to ask for if missing

- Type: raid, giveaway, rank requirement, milestone, event, or revamp.
- The core detail (what, when, where, prize, requirement).
- Which game section, if relevant (Cursed Blade, Sailor Piece, Universal Tower Defense, Grow a Garden 2).
- Any role to ping (state it, do not invent role names).

## Voice rules (hard)

- Guild voice: competitive, hype, community-driven. Tagline "Zenith of Power."
- Never use "delve", "leverage", "fantastic", or em dashes.
- Short lines. One idea per line. Specific numbers.
- Open with a hook, close with a punch.
- Use Discord markdown: bold headers, bullet points, a clear call to action.

## Output

Give 3 versions. Label the strongest and say why in one line.
Keep it scannable on mobile. Suggest 1 or 2 emojis max per section, not a wall of them.

## Guardrail

Draft only. Never post. Chris reviews and posts himself.
