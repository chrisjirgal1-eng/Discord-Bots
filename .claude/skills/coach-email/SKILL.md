# Coach outreach email

Draft a recruiting email to a college track and field coach for Chris (D1 recruitment).

## Inputs to ask for if missing

- Coach name and school.
- Event(s) and current personal bests (PRs), with units.
- Grad year and GPA if he wants it included.
- Any specific hook (visited the campus, watched the team, a recent result).

## Voice rules (hard)

- Natural, human, competitive. Not robotic.
- Never use "delve", "leverage", "fantastic", or em dashes.
- Short lines. One idea per line. Specific numbers over vague claims.
- Open with a hook, close with a punch.
- No filler praise. No "I hope this email finds you well."
- Sound like a driven 17-year-old athlete, not a press release.

## Structure

1. One-line hook: who he is, event, headline PR.
2. Two or three lines: why this program, specific and real.
3. The numbers: PRs, grad year, GPA if included.
4. Ask: a clear next step (film link, call, camp, visit).
5. Sign-off with full name and contact.

## Output

Give 3 versions. Label the strongest and say why in one line.
Keep each under 150 words. Texas-priority schools get a sharper "why your program" line.

## Guardrail

Draft only. Never send. Chris reviews and sends himself.
