## Core loop & progression (confirmed by Chris)

1. **Ding-dong-ditch houses.** Ring the bell, grab or steal the anime girl, run.
2. **Place stolen anime girls on your base.** Each one generates Cash/Sec.
3. **Rarer girls pay more Cash/Sec**, pulled via RNG rolls.
4. **Roll for different base styles.** Each base style gives a **cash multiplier**.
5. Progression scale on the thumbnail: **10$/sec early rising to 973,282,881$/sec
   late** (millions to billions per second at endgame).

The engine is the stack: steal, then base income, then RNG rarity, then base-style
multiplier. Closest system neighbors to study: Steal a Brainrot (steal-from-others
loop), Grow a Garden (idle Cash/Sec plus collect), Pet Simulator and Bubble Gum
Simulator (RNG rarity plus multipliers).

Open on mechanics **(confirm)**: is stealing PvP (from other live players' bases) or
PvE (NPC houses)? Is there base defense / anti-steal, trading, or rebirth / prestige?
These shape the economy and the event design.

## The bar (reference games, why each works, do not copy)

Chris's study list, with the specific lever each one pulls:

- **Anime Expeditions:** the model he screenshotted. Event cards with a "Happening
  now" badge plus Join Event button, emoji-hook description, Store link. 97% likes,
  209K active. Lever: the page looks live and curated, event-first.
- **Anime Rangers X:** every update ships a new meta unit. Lever: chase-the-new-unit FOMO.
- **Anime Last Stand:** constant limited banners plus collab units. Lever: scarcity plus collab reach.
- **Steal a Brainrot:** steal loop plus social griefing. Lever: PvP theft drives emergent drama and clips.
- **Grow a Garden:** idle Cash/Sec plus weather/limited seed drops, huge CCU. Lever: check-back idle plus timed drops.
- **Blox Fruits:** long-haul progression plus big named updates. Lever: update-as-event, roadmap trust.
- **Blue Lock Rivals:** sport-anime collab timing plus competitive meta. Lever: ride the anime's airing hype.
- **Bubble Gum Simulator:** RNG pets plus multipliers plus luck events. Lever: 2x luck weekends print engagement.
- **Anime Defenders:** limited units on a countdown plus evolve system. Lever: countdown-gated limiteds.
- **Pet Simulator:** RNG rarity ladder plus huge/exclusive pets plus trading. Lever: rarity flex plus trade economy.
- **Blade Ball:** tight skill loop plus frequent balance plus cosmetic drops. Lever: fast cadence keeps it fresh.

Pattern across all of them: something new is always visibly happening, and the page
communicates it before a player even clicks Play.

## The goal (the whole point)

The game page must **always look alive**. A player landing on it should think
"something new is happening here," never "this hasn't updated in months." Every
system Cowork designs serves that one job: thumbnails, event cards, countdowns,
secondary images, and announcements, all coordinated so the page reads as
professionally planned months ahead.
