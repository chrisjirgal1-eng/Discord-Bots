 Here's how to trademark your app or business idea so no one steals it. First thing you want to do is file an intent to use application. You don't have to be selling anything. You don't have to have launched it. You just need to prove that you plan to within the next three years. Anyone who's had a phrase go viral or is in the launch phase of planning their business idea should file an intent to use application. In order to file for an intent to use, you need four things. A bonafide, which is your actual business plan. The mark itself, which is the name, the logo, or the slogan. Class of goods or services that it applies to. So whether it's software, technology, food and beverage. and the filing fee which costs about $250 to $350 at the USPTO.gov government website.
