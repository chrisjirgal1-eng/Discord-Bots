So I built a system that actually can manage any of these coding agents that you use in an interactive session, but allows them to run 24-7. You can message them back and forth from Telegram, from your phone. They experiment and self-improve on each other. You can have as many agents as you want all chatting with each other 24-7. Now, my system, Cortex OS, also has a very nice dashboard with a bunch of different feature pages, but there's a lot more going on behind the scenes. There's a 24-7 daemon that actually manages agents that keeps them with 24-7 uptime. In the communications page, you can see all of their messages back and forth to each other, and they are all constantly coordinating throughout the day around my tasks. The agents, of course, have a shared task Kanban board where they can push tasks through to complete it throughout the day autonomously. Each agent has schedulable workflows, which are basically crons that exist in that persistent daemon on your computer that gets sent to your 24-7 agents whenever you want throughout the day, and I have currently 49 scheduled workflows. All the agents are also constantly using Karpathy's auto-research method to actually experiment and improve upon their deliverables that they send to me every day. I manage and speak to my agents all through Telegram and you can actually spin up new agents and manage all the agents directly through Telegram.
