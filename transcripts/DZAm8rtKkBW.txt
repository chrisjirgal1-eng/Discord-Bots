 At 14, I made six figures in three months as a top Roblox developer. Here are the top three things that I learned. Number one, be different. There are thousands of games all across the Roblox front page. And if you don't separate yourself out from the rest, you'll never succeed. Number two, focus on what the players actually want. If you don't implement bug fixes or features that players are requesting, then you're going to have horrible retention and players will never come back. Number three, be passionate. Maintaining a game with millions of players takes huge amounts of effort. And if you don't truly enjoy what you're doing, you're going to burn out very quickly.
