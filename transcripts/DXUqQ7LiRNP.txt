 Every token you send leaves a trail. That trail doesn't shrink. It grows five times bigger. Every prompt saves two vectors per token. A key. A value. That's the KV cache. At 32,000 tokens on Lama70B, it's 17 gigabytes. Per request. Every new token makes it bigger, so engineers quantize. Squeeze the numbers down. But 3-bit always broke accuracy. Google's trick. Rewrite each vector in polar form. Radius and angle. The angles follow a known pattern so you only keep a few bits. That's polar quant. A Johnson-Linden Strauss rotation handles the rest, with sine bits. Three bits per number instead of 16. Five times less memory. No fine tuning, no calibration, drops into any transformer. Compress the cache, keep the accuracy. Ship longer context.
