🚨 The head of Claude Code at Anthropic says he doesn't prompt Claude anymore

Speaking at the Acquired Unplugged event in San Francisco, Boris Cherny said his daily work has changed. "I don't prompt Claude anymore. I have loops running that prompt Claude and figuring out what to do. My job is to write loops."

A year ago he wrote code by hand. Then he ran 5 to 10 Claude sessions at once, prompting each himself. Now he builds automated loops that feed Claude a task, let it act, check the result, and decide what to do next until the work is done.

Old workflows put the developer at the keyboard, typing one prompt and reading the output before the next. The loop removes that step. 

The system does the prompting, and the person designs the system. Cherny said this is the shift he expects to define the rest of the year.
